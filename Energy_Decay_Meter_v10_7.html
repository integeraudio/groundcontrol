Before you start — three things to check:
1. Turn on your studio monitors or subwoofer and connect them as the default audio output.
2. Place a measurement mic (e.g. UMIK-1) at your mix position at ear level.
3. Keep the room quiet during the sweep — no fans, no HVAC if possible. Your browser will request microphone permission when you click Connect & Calibrate.
⚠ Measurement Limitations:
• Hybrid T60 — Schroeder below 50Hz (more accurate), CSD above 50Hz (better time resolution).
• Single position — Results are position-dependent. ISO 3382 recommends spatial averaging.
• User-selectable sweeps — 2 sweeps default (faster), 4 sweeps for best SNR at low frequencies.
• Electrical contamination — Narrow peaks >1.0s may indicate AC hum or interference.
• Subwoofer contribution — Low-frequency decay includes speaker ringing, not just room modes.

About This Test

This report measures how long bass energy lingers in the room after a tone stops, across the 20–80 Hz range. The user-selectable number of sweeps (1, 2, 4, or 8) are pip-aligned and averaged together before deconvolution to improve low-frequency signal-to-noise. A hybrid T60 method is used: Schroeder backward integration below 50Hz (accurate for low frequencies) and fixed 250ms FFT-based CSD above 50Hz (better time resolution).

Method: Hybrid T60 — Schroeder <50Hz, CSD >50Hz. Narrow peaks >1.0s are flagged as potential electrical contamination.
