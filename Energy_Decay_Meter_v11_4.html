Before you start — four things to check:
1. Turn on your studio monitors or subwoofer and connect them as the default audio output.
2. Place a measurement mic (e.g. UMIK-1) at your mix position at ear level.
3. Keep the room quiet during the sweep — no fans, no HVAC if possible.
4. For a valid before/after, do not move the mic or change the playback volume between the Baseline and Nodal 30 sweeps. The bass field changes over a few centimetres, so any movement or level change invalidates the comparison. Your browser will request microphone permission when you click Connect & Calibrate.

About This Test

This report measures how long bass energy lingers in the room after a tone stops, across the 20–80 Hz range. A Schroeder backward integration method is used with 1 Hz frequency resolution (61 points).

Method: Schroeder backward integration, adaptive T30/T20/T10 fit (whichever the usable dynamic range supports), extrapolated to 60 dB. The average reduction uses only bands measured reliably in both the before and after sweeps. The dashed line on the chart is the analysis filter's own ring-down floor (≈0.48 s at 20 Hz falling to ≈0.12 s at 80 Hz); values at or below it are limited by the measurement, not the room, and are excluded. Narrow peaks >1.0 s are flagged as potential contamination.
