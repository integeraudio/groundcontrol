This tool tells you two things: whether your room has a bass decay problem, and whether your Nodal 30 units fixed it. Click Connect Microphone to begin. The test plays short bass tones through your speakers at five frequencies across the 25–60 Hz range, then goes silent. Any energy the mic picks up during the silence is your room ringing out — bass that should have stopped, but didn't. The numbers you see are how long that ringing lasts, in seconds. The professional target is 0.25–0.55 seconds.

About This Test

This report shows how long bass energy lingers in your room after a tone stops, measured across five frequencies from 25–60 Hz. The target for professional monitoring environments is 0.25–0.55 seconds (T60). Times above 0.75 seconds cause audible problems: bloated kick drums, uneven bass across the room, and mixes that don't translate. The Nodal 30 is designed to reduce decay times across the full 20–60 Hz range.
